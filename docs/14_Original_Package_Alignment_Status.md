## Explicit differences to preserve

- **Autosave is required by original document 01 but is not implemented.** Current saves create append-only clinical revisions. A separate draft/autosave contract is needed before implementation; explicit saves are a documented scope gap, not full equivalence.
- **Viewer:** authorized PACS metadata/frame access and linking are narrower than an integrated diagnostic viewer. M06/M07 must establish secure integration and geometry/performance/clinical acceptance.
- **AI:** the current Verbatim adapter preserves clinician-checked text. It is not the package's full asynchronous AI job or clinical generation capability. Manual reporting stays available.
- **State/API compatibility:** actual order/report state machines and routes are retained. PendingReview does not mean a second opinion; addenda are linked reports; signed export is plain text. Import/processing/delivery states cannot be inferred from metadata links.
- **Security and acceptance:** development authentication and application tenant controls are implemented, but institutional identity/session/MFA deployment and production verification remain M02/release work. Existing hash integrity is not PKI signing or administrator-proof audit.
- **v2 is additive:** M05B implements optional indication/technique and saved section provenance. Document extraction, source-side review and future extraction citations remain M08-M10; manual/checked-text attribution does not claim extraction provenance.
